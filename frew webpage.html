The Lost Wallet One sunny afternoon, Mark was walking home from school when he noticed a wallet lying beside the road. He picked it up and found some money and an identification card inside. Instead of keeping it, Mark decided to find the owner. He followed the address on the card and went to a small house nearby. An old man opened the door and was surprised to see his lost wallet. He thanked Mark and told him that the money was needed to buy medicine. Mark felt happy because he had done the right thing. From that day on, Mark learned that honesty and kindness can make a big difference in someone's life.
