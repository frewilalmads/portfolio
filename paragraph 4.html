One afternoon, while walking home from school, Mark noticed a small wallet lying beside the road. He picked it up and found some money and an identification card inside. Instead of keeping it, Mark decided to find the owner. He followed the address on the card and eventually met an old man who was worried because he had lost his wallet. The man was very thankful when Mark returned it. Mark went home feeling happy because he had done the right thing. From that day on, he learned that honesty is more valuable than money.
